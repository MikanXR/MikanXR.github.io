# Lens calibration with a phone

MikanTrack measures each camera's lens by watching a pattern from several angles. A single-camera setup needs nothing printed: the pattern is a chessboard shown on a phone screen.

## Showing the pattern

Open the [calibration pattern page](pathname:///calibrate/) on the phone. The lens calibration step in MikanTrack shows a QR code that leads to the same page.

The page draws the chessboard full screen and keeps the screen awake. Tap it once to hide the instructions and go full screen where the phone allows it. The phone can be held in either orientation.

If the phone has no connection, use the export button in the calibration step. It writes the pattern as an image to send to the phone and show full screen.

## Capturing

- Hold the phone 20 to 30 cm from the camera with the whole pattern in view.

- Keep lamps from reflecting on the screen. If the pattern is not found, lower the screen brightness.

- Hold still until a capture is taken, then move or tilt the phone and hold still again. Twelve captures are needed.

- Tilt the phone clearly toward or away from the camera for several captures. Focal length can only be measured from perspective, so captures that all face the camera flat cannot calibrate it.

## Multi-camera rigs

A rig with more than one camera uses the printed charuco board by default. The phone pattern can be chosen there as well.
