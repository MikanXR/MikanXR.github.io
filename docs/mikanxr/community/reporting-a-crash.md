# Reporting a crash

When MikanXR crashes it writes a report to `Documents\MikanXR\CrashReports` and offers to open that folder the next time it starts. Attach the report's `.dmp`, `.txt`, and `.log` files to a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/MikanXR/MikanXR/issues) along with what you were doing. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
